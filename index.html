    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    How accurate is it?

    Hylens recognises the foods and estimates the portions from your photo. It’s an estimate: you can correct any food or enter its exact weight in two taps and everything updates.

    Tip: good light and a photo taken from above give the best results.

    Why track salt and potassium?

    Salt makes your body hold on to water, and potassium helps balance it. Following both at each meal helps you understand your hydration, not just count glasses.

    What happens to my photos?

    Your photo is sent to our analysis service for the calculation, then Hylens doesn’t keep it. Your meals and your history stay on your phone. Details in our privacy policy.

    How much does it cost?

    Nothing during the closed beta. At launch, Hylens Premium is €9.99/month or €34.99/year. The first 100 subscribers keep €4.99/month or €29.99/year for as long as they stay subscribed.

    Is it available on iPhone?

    Not yet: the beta starts on Android. Send us a message and we’ll tell you as soon as the iPhone version is out.

    Do I need a smartwatch?

    No. With your permission, Health Connect adds your steps and workouts automatically. Without it, you log your workouts by hand.

    Is it medical advice?

    No. Hylens gives estimates and general wellness guidance. For any health question, talk to a professional.
