    FAQ

    Before you download.

    Is it accurate?

    Values come from official food tables: Ciqual (ANSES) in France, USDA elsewhere. Portions are estimated from the photo: it’s an estimate, not a weigh-in, and you can correct every food.

    Where do photos go in the app?

    Your photo is sent to our analysis service, powered by OpenAI, and Hylens doesn’t keep it. In the app, your meals and tracking stay on your phone.

    How much does it cost?

    The beta is free, no credit card. A founder rate with limited spots will be available at launch.

    Do I need Health Connect or a watch?

    No. With your permission, Health Connect adds your steps and workouts automatically. Without it, you log workouts by hand.

    What about iPhone?

    Not yet. Hylens is in beta on Android; the iPhone version comes next.

    Is this medical advice?

    No. Hylens provides estimates and wellness guidelines. For any health question, talk to a professional.
